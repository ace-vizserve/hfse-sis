# Admission Process

## Part 1

1. **Overview**
   - Book your Open House Schedule at HFSE International School.

2. **Application**
   - Fill out Online Enrolment Form.

3. **Documents Submission**
   - Submit all Admission Forms and Supporting Documents.

4. **Registration**
   - Pay the Registration and Application Fees.
   - School Fees
   - Payment via Bank Transfer

5. **Assessment**
   - Schedule an assessment with Admissions.
   - Assessment is conducted on Tuesdays, 9AM, at 223@Mountbatten and must be taken 2 weeks before enrolment date.

6. **Interview**
   - Attend a 5-10 Minute Interview with Academics Team.

## Part 2

7. **Documents Verification**
   - The Admissions Officer will verify that all submitted documents are complete and up-to-date.

8. **Assessment Outcome**
   - Release of assessment and interview results.
   - Students identified as having Additional Learning Needs (ALN), whether declared or observed, must attend Trial Classes. These sessions allow for a comprehensive assessment of their needs and capabilities before final recommendations are made.

9. **Contract Signing**
   - Sign Student Contract.

10. **Enrolment**

- Pay Enrolment Fees.
- School Fees
- Payment Method

11. **Class Assignment**

- The Student Affairs Officers will assign section on a first come, first served basis and subject to a deliberation by Academics Team.

12. **Collection of Supplies**

- Schedule will be announced.

13. **Attend Parents' Orientation**

- Schedule will be announced.

## Disclaimer

Students who did not pass the examination and/or with declaration, must attend Trial Class + SPED and/or will be under conditional enrolment.
